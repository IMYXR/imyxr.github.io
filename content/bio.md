I'm a PhD candidate in Department of Computer Science NC State, supervised by [Dr. Qiao Jin](https://georgiejin.com/). My research focus on how human collaborate with LLM agents in extended reality (XR). To be specific, I design spatial interfaces and agentic workflow that help users understand, supervise, and correct what AI agents plan and say. My work published in ACM CHI, IEEE TVCG, IEEE VL/HCC.
